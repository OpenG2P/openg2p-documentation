# Exchange

The [Exchange](architecture/exchange.md) is where partners onboard once and use one farmer consent across registries. Most of its components are OpenG2P products, documented with them: the [Consent Manager](../consent-management/README.md) (in the exchange role, with its partner portal), [Partner Management](../platform/platform-services/partner-management/README.md) and eSignet (the farmer's [national-ID login](../consent-management/design/consent-collection.md#national-id-authentication-in-the-partner-portal)). The Agri Stack–specific component is the [Composite](composite/README.md), documented below. Why the Exchange works the way it does: [The Exchange](architecture/exchange.md); installing it: [Deployment: Exchange](deployment/exchange.md).
