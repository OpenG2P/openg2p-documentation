# Administration

| Guide | What it does | Who |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Managing Partners](managing-partners.md) | Onboard a partner, assign and remove its use cases, disable it, give its staff partner portal accounts | The Exchange's partner manager; portal accounts, its Keycloak administrator |
| [Collecting a Consent](collecting-a-consent.md) | Record a farmer's consent in the partner portal and approve it | Partner staff; a Consent Approver at the Exchange |
| [Releasing Registry Data to the Composite](releasing-data-to-the-composite.md) | Set what a department's registry releases to the Composite, and change it as use cases change | Each department's partner manager |
| [Keys](keys.md) | Replace or rotate a signing key; respond to a compromised key | Each install's operator |
