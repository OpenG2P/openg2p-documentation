# Reporting & Dashboards

PBMS offers comprehensive reporting and dashboard capabilities through Apache Superset. Pre-built reports cover key areas such as transactions, program statistics, and reconciliation status, giving program managers immediate visibility into operations. In addition, implementation teams can build custom dashboards and reports to meet specific requirements. While Superset is the default reporting tool, PBMS is designed flexibly so that other business intelligence tools can also be used for visualization and analysis.
