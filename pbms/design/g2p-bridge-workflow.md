# Post disbursement workflow

The disbursement list, once approved for disbursement, is handed over to G2P Bridge for further downstream processing.

{% embed url="https://miro.com/app/board/uXjVJflnNxg=/?share_link_id=354101456363" %}

G2P Bridge receives Envelopes and Disbursements (organized in batches) under these envelopes. G2P Bridge then processes these disbursements. The detailed process flow within G2P Bridge can be found [here](https://docs.openg2p.org/g2p-bridge/features/cash-goods-and-services).
