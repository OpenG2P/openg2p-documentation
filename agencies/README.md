# Agencies

The **Agency Solution** lets a department onboard and govern field-level partners (NGOs, business correspondents, cooperatives, local-government outreach teams), let each agency manage its own agents, and let those agents do enumeration and disbursement work, online or offline, from an Android tablet. Every action stays within the data scope the department intended.

It spans IAM, Registry, G2P Bridge and the Android app.
