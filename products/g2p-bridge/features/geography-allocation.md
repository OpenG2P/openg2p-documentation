# Geography Allocation

#### Resolve Beneficiary Geography

Automatically determine the geographic location (state/district/block) of each beneficiary.

#### Support Geographic Hierarchy

Organize geography in hierarchy: Country → State → District → Block.

#### Group by Large Geographic Areas

Group disbursements by large geographic zones (states/provinces) for warehouse coordination.

#### Group by Small Geographic Areas

Group disbursements by small geographic zones (districts/blocks) for agency coordination.

#### Track Geographic Allocation

Maintain record of which geographic zone each beneficiary belongs to.
