# Agency Allocation

#### Allocate Delivery Agencies

Assign delivery agencies to geographic zones based on capability and authorization.

#### Respect Agency Authorization

Only allocate agencies that are authorized to deliver specific program/benefit combinations.

#### Respect Agency Geographic Coverage

Only allocate agencies that serve the beneficiary's geographic zone.

#### Distribute Among Eligible Agencies

When multiple agencies are eligible, select one for balanced distribution.

#### Capture Agency Contact Details

Record agency administrator name, email, and phone for official notifications.

#### Support Agency Attributes

Store and manage additional agency attributes (capacity, specialization, etc.).
