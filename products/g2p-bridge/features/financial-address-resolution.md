# Financial address resolution

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/G2P Bridge Account Mapper Resolution.jpg" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

#### Resolve Bank Accounts

Look up beneficiary bank account details (account number, bank code, branch code).

#### Resolve Mobile Wallets

Look up beneficiary mobile wallet information (phone number, wallet provider).

#### Resolve Email Wallets

Look up beneficiary email wallet details (email address, email provider).

#### Support Multiple Address Types

Support resolution of any of the above address types in a single operation.

#### Determine Preferred Payment Method

Based on resolved addresses, determine the best payment method for each beneficiary.

#### Cache Resolved Addresses

Store resolved addresses for future reference and audit purposes.
